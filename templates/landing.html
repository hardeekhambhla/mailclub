How it arrives

Sent, not emailed

Every issue goes out on the 1st of the month, by post, not email, on purpose. I wanted this to land in your mailbox, not sit in a tab you forget to close. Figure on it showing up around the 15th, give or take however long the postal system feels like taking that week.

I ship internationally too. Outside India, it just takes your envelope a little longer to find you.

The philosophy

Why I started this

I read more autobiographies than novels. I find it so interesting to see what people have gone through, and how distinct yet similar all of our life experiences are.

I've always wanted to get better at telling stories myself.

So this is my attempt at that. I'm going looking for those stories, through archives, one at a time. Turns out I'm not the only one who wants them.

You don't have to go far to end up somewhere else entirely.
Sometimes it just takes opening an envelope.
